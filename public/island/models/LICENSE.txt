Most 3D models in this folder are by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com) and are
released under CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Packs used (converted to GLB, animations trimmed, meshopt-compressed):
- Ultimate Nature Pack
- Ultimate Crops Pack
- Ultimate Animated Animals
- Simple Buildings Pack (House.glb: roof color of the palette texture edited to purple)
- Farm Buildings Pack
- Cute Fish Pack (fish, FishingRod_Lvl1, Dock_Long, Boat)

Thank you, Quaternius! https://www.patreon.com/quaternius

Wear_*.glb (hats and glasses) are CC0 models from OpenGameArt.org, converted
from FBX to GLB with textures dropped and recolored in code:
- Wear_Straw, Wear_Cloche, Wear_Beret, Wear_HardHat, Wear_Elf, Wear_Grad,
  Wear_TopHat, Wear_Antler, Wear_Headphones: "Hats, Clothing, & Props" by Teh_Bucket
  https://opengameart.org/content/hats-clothing-props
- Wear_Crown: "Low Poly Crown" by BullHornGames
  https://opengameart.org/content/low-poly-crown
- Wear_GlassesRound, Wear_GlassesVisor, Wear_GlassesPixel: "Lowpoly Glasses Pack" by iPoly3D
  https://opengameart.org/content/lowpoly-glasses-pack
